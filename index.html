Featured case

Capstone · Machine Learning Track

Refresh / Content Opportunity Scoring

A prioritization model that helps content teams decide which pages deserve review first — built from a hand-written baseline, checked for leakage twice, and compared against trained models on the exact same held-out data.

Headline result: the hand-written baseline actually scored worse than random selection on the final target (Precision@50 of 0.040 vs. a 0.232 base rate) — both trained models clearly beat it once evaluated fairly.
