About

I'm a Machine Learning intern at FlyRank, working across the ML and AI Fluency tracks. I got into this because I like the moment a messy pile of data turns into a decision someone can actually act on — not a black-box score, something you can open up and argue with.

Before I trust a model, I want to know what it's leaning on and where it's wrong. That's why every project on this site starts with a baseline I can beat honestly, and a data contract naming exactly what could quietly leak the answer. I'd rather report an uncomfortable number than a clean one I can't fully explain.
