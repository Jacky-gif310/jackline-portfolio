Bio Projects Skills Connect

I'm a Machine Learning Intern at FlyRank, working across the Machine Learning and General AI Fluency tracks. I got into this because I enjoy the moment when a messy pile of data becomes a decision someone can actually act on — not a black-box score, but something you can open, question, and improve.

Before I trust a model, I want to understand what it is leaning on and where it can be wrong. That's why my projects start with transparent baselines, careful validation, and explicit checks for data leakage. I'd rather report an uncomfortable number than a clean one I cannot fully explain.

Through the FlyRank internship, I've worked on content opportunity scoring, baseline ranking systems, feature engineering, model evaluation, AI-assisted research workflows, and technical documentation.
