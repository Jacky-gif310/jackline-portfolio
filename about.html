About

Building practical machine learning systems that turn messy search data into clear, evidence-based decisions.

I'm a Machine Learning Intern at FlyRank, working across both the Machine Learning and General AI Fluency tracks. My work focuses on building decision-support systems that transform scattered search performance signals into ranked, explainable recommendations that content teams can confidently act on.

What interests me most isn't simply training models—it's understanding why they make a recommendation. Before trusting any model, I build transparent baselines, validate the underlying signals, document assumptions, and check carefully for data leakage. I believe machine learning earns trust when every result can be explained, challenged, and improved.

Through the FlyRank internship I've worked on content opportunity scoring, baseline ranking systems, feature engineering, model evaluation, AI-assisted research workflows, and technical documentation. Every project on this portfolio reflects one principle:

"Evidence before confidence."
