About

I'm a Machine Learning intern at FlyRank, working across the ML and AI Fluency tracks. My focus is decision-support modeling — systems that help a person prioritize and decide, rather than systems that quietly decide for them. I care about a model being able to explain itself: what it leans on, where it's wrong, and what it still can't prove.

Before any model, I write down the baseline it has to beat and the ways the data could quietly leak the answer — that discipline shows up throughout the case study on this site.
